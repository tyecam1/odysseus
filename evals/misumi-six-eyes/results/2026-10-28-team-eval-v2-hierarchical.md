# Six Eyes on the -11 team evaluation v2 (real completed-task evidence), cluster-aware

Generated by `evals/misumi-six-eyes/from_team_eval.py` from `evals/misumi-team/eval-v2/data/` (192 replies per condition = 8 distinct conflict/clean task pairs x 12 repeated runs x 2 prompt kinds). Deterministic: the bootstrap is seeded. Supersedes `2026-10-28-team-eval-v2.md`, which resampled the runs as if each were an independent task.

## What this can and cannot say

- **8 distinct tasks, many runs.** Repeated runs of one task are clustered. Intervals resample tasks and then runs; the evidence floor counts distinct tasks and 20 are required, so no verdict is established here. Readings labelled indicative are for orientation and never a basis for a routing or policy change.
- Only a solo-versus-team question over one task family (household planning) with turn latency as the only recorded cost. No tokens, paid cost or compute seconds were recorded, so none are compared; nothing is summed across units.
- **Safety was not measured.** The evaluation defined no safety floor beyond correctness, so safety is unmeasured (`safety_ok` is None). The absence of a violation count here is not evidence of safety.
- `teamfix` and `teamdrop` were chosen after seeing the `team` data (post-hoc) on the same tasks: not independent confirmation.
- The two blind judges agree poorly (kappa 0.34 and 0.14 in RESULTS.md), and the more accurate one (qwen3:8b, 94% agreement with the 48-row AI audit) is the same model family as the subject. Treat a reading as stable only where both judges give the same one; the disagreement is tabulated at the end.
- Not covered: a cheap-first escalating baseline, a strong-solo-plus-verifier baseline, a deterministic-tool plan and the optimiser itself; there are no real matched records for them yet. Team size is not a success metric.
