# Capture rationale

Combines causal throughput optimisation with an explicit anti-sprawl bias: measure completed outcomes, retire unused features and ship bounded simplifications while retaining the single routing/authority system.

## Reusable behaviours

- Target accepted outcomes per scarce allowance and operator intervention, not misleading raw token-count optimisation.
- Separate cheap evidence collection from premium cross-repository arbitration and low-cost implementation.
- Require one real end-to-end trace, small reversible PRs, and measured before/after verification.
- Treat not building, retiring or simplifying a feature as potentially better than integration.

## Assumptions and limitations

- No estimate of savings is established until telemetry and actual post-change execution support it.
- Existing repository authorities, qualifying routes, security, physical-safety, ethics and research-signoff gates remain binding.
- Candidate exemplar only, not a registered or activated initialiser.
