# Capture rationale

Shifts optimization from prompt-count cosmetology to delivered outcomes, with an explicit counterfactual measurement design and a keep-wire-retire integration gate.

## Reusable behaviours

- Separate token-volume proxies, allowance consumption and verified delivery throughput.
- Test cost hypotheses against existing telemetry and acceptance quality before recommending changes.
- Do not integrate unused subsystems for the sake of integration.

## Task-specific assumptions / limitations

- No new scheduler, router or prompt runtime.
- Do not alter backlog precedence, protected approval, paid-provider qualification or review obligations.
