## Why Stage 0 is the stopping point

A prospective shadow job needs stable evidence identities to distinguish changed evidence, legitimate retries and repeated review. The database can join recent activation and execution, and finalisation can supply the commit. The sampled durable data cannot join each independent verdict and test run to the specific task revision, reviewed artifact and evidence revision. It also cannot establish negative coverage across the three owners or independently replay the sampled review decisions. Adding a collector before fixing those joins would risk silently flagging valid review, treating recovered leases as accepted work, or misattributing provider failures. Qualified independent verification of this baseline remains owed under the owning card.

The smallest remediation is to resolve access to the existing authorized lab review/status route and obtain one sanitized Odysseus-only receipt set covering an unchanged-artifact recheck, a changed-artifact recheck and a genuine no-fault control. Attach to existing stage/trace receipts the already known owner/task/revision, execution and artifact SHA, plus source artifact reference, test run or local-run receipt ID, review ID, motive, reviewer identity/family and the evidence revision used. Keep event and ingestion times distinct. Reuse trace/span IDs where present and retain an explicit unknown otherwise. Bind review debt to its exact evidence revision and let the existing owners decide whether it is settled. Capture provenance and gate status independently. Use IDs and bounded metadata, with prompt and secret content excluded. Recheck when the lab route and attributable receipts are available, then ask the qualified independent lane to verify the sampling and joins through the controller. The missing review is a delivery dependency.

The report's weekly summary uses the existing result document. Collector, held-out evaluation, shadow canary and rollback exercises are deferred by the acceptance stop rule. No observer behavior was enabled. The current rollback exposure is the report itself. Any later shadow deployment needs its own independent review, opt-in flag-off rollback proof, and ordinary merge, release and real-path evidence. The owner card and prompt traces are left to the controller.

## Research foundations

- Google SRE postmortems motivate source-linked contributing conditions and corrective actions. CAST offers incident analysis across interacting controls, and STPA applies to prospective hazards. For this audit, lease, runner, test and review controls warrant their own evidence. [Google SRE Postmortem Culture](https://sre.google/workbook/postmortem-culture/), [MIT CAST and STPA handbooks](https://www.mit.stamp-institute.com/handbooks)
- SPACE supports measuring effort, collaboration, flow and outcomes across different dimensions. DORA supplies delivery and instability concepts at the software/service level. Per-task lead time and avoidable re-verification need local definitions and stable denominators. [SPACE](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-space-of-developer-productivity-theres-more-to-it-than-you-think/), [DORA metrics](https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics/)
- OpenTelemetry supplies trace/span relationships and compatible GenAI attributes where the runtime has them. Application identities, versions and owner acceptance remain domain-specific. [OTel traces](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/traces/), [OTel GenAI conventions](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/gen-ai/)
- Wang et al. survey trajectory-based attribution methods. Step ordering and agent explanations leave room for competing mechanisms, particularly with partial logs. AgentTelemetry's controlled taxonomy suggests fields worth testing. Its reported +12.5 percentage-point matched intervention result was statistically inconclusive (Fisher's exact `p=0.53`, `n=24`). An effect claim for Odysseus needs a matched or prospective canary with missed-defect and burden measurements. [Wang et al., IEEE TSE](https://www.computer.org/csdl/journal/ts/2026/11/11626967/2iuF5rxPMqs), [AgentTelemetry, ACM](https://doi.org/10.1145/3805760.3814931)
